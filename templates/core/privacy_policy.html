Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you browse, register, or make a purchase on BrandBazaar, we collect your name, email address, phone number, and physical shipping address to fulfill orders and prevent fraudulent activity.

2. Protection of Customer Data

All sensitive passwords are encrypted using state-of-the-art PBKDF2 with SHA-256 password hashing. We never store raw credit/debit card numbers or CVVs on our databases.

We share your shipping information solely with licensed logistics partners for package delivery and never sell your personal contact information to third-party advertisers.
