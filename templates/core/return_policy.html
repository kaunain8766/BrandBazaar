Returns & Refund Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Return Window

BrandBazaar offers a straightforward 7-day return policy from the date of physical delivery for eligible categories (Apparel, Footwear, Fashion Accessories).

2. Eligibility Conditions
  • Items must be unused, unwashed, and in the original condition with brand tags attached.
  • Shoes and sneakers must be tried indoors and returned with the intact manufacturer shoebox.
  • Beauty, personal care, and cosmetic items are non-returnable once the protective seal is broken for hygienic reasons.
3. Refund Process

Once our fulfillment hub inspects and approves the returned item, refunds are credited back to the original payment source within 5 to 7 business days. For COD orders, store credit or NEFT bank transfer details will be requested.
