Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Order Processing Time

All standard orders are processed and handed over to our verified courier partners within 1 to 2 business days (excluding Sundays and national holidays).

2. Shipping Charges

BrandBazaar currently offers Free Shipping on all domestic prepaid and Cash on Delivery (COD) orders across India. Any promotional shipping terms are clearly displayed at checkout.

3. Estimated Transit Times
  • Metro Cities (Delhi-NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata): 2 to 4 business days
  • Tier-2 & Tier-3 Cities: 3 to 6 business days
  • Remote Areas / North East / J&K: 5 to 8 business days
4. Order Tracking

Once your package is confirmed, you can track its live progress in real-time from the My Orders section of your BrandBazaar account.
